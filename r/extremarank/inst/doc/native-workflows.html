Native R audits, coverage and practical workflows

ExtremaRank runs directly in R through its C++ exact kernel. Python is not a runtime dependency. Native certificates and observed fitted-model sensitivity have different scopes. Search caps leave unresolved questions explicit.

Rows in effects are donors, columns are features. For ordinary extremarank input the orientation is features by samples. Preparation, feature filtering and ranking ties are frozen before deletion. A lower change bound of budget+1 means stability within the budget; it does not prove that the next deletion cardinality causes a change.

Storage estimates exclude model objects and arbitrary precision integer memory. max_dense_bytes guards prepared sample matrices and pseudobulk output. DelayedMatrix inputs are read in row blocks; native audits still materialize their smaller feature-by-biological-unit matrix. Pseudobulk aggregation does not materialize the entire delayed cell matrix. Audit and diagnostic limits are independent. Default diagnostics are skipped at budget zero and otherwise capped at 100; use diagnostics="all" with an explicit limit when needed.

NOT_EVALUATED means no deletion was run. PARTIALLY_EVALUATED records an incomplete stable-looking run. OBSERVED_CHANGED includes actual change witnesses, even if other scenarios failed. None certifies unseen refits.

For larger models use store_scores="disk", score_path="scores" or store_scores="none". checkpoint="fits-cache" saves each completed fit; resume=TRUE validates its frozen contract and can extend max_refits. workers=2 runs two PSOCK processes and collects results in scenario order. Processes can increase RAM; the numeric input-copy guard does not bound model working memory. delete_by="site" deletes whole sites. Covariates and contrasts are fixed even if a deletion makes the design unidentifiable.

Custom repeated-measure or multi-group models can use refit_extremarank_custom. Its function must refit the supplied retained data and return every frozen feature exactly once, with feature_id, score and effect. Declare biological deletion units and control any external state. This interface has an observed sensitivity scope.

Real microarray, single-cell, protein and lipid reproductions, including failed fits, are recorded in the repository’s results/v04 and results/v05 folders. The package does not establish FDR control or biological validity.
